Delete your Spark Chain data

Spark Chain (by Pitchcraft Labs) does not require an account and does not keep player data on our own servers. Here is how to remove everything:

1. Game progress on your phone

Open the game → Settings → Reset progress (tap twice), or simply uninstall the app. This deletes levels, stars, coins and settings.

2. Leaderboard scores & achievements (Google Play Games)

Open the Google Play Games app (or Play Store → your profile picture → Play Games) → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data → find Spark Chain → Delete. To only hide yourself, use Settings → Profile and privacy.

3. Ask us

Email pitchcraftlabs@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – Spark Chain”. We reply and help within 30 days.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.
