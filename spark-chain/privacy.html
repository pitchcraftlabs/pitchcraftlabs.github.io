Privacy Policy

App: Spark Chain (com.pitchcraftlabs.sparkchain) · Developer: Pitchcraft Labs

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pitchcraft Labs (“we”, “us”) handles information when you play Spark Chain (the “Game”) on Android. We built the Game to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect ourselves

Playing does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, lives and settings) is stored on your device. We do not run our own servers or databases for player data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewarded-video completions)
  • crash and diagnostic data relating to the ads SDK

This processing is done by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Google Play Games (leaderboards and achievements)

If you use Google Play Games on your phone, the Game signs you in automatically (or when you tap Sign in with Play Games). Google then provides your Play Games gamer name, avatar and player ID to the Game, and the Game sends your total stars, highest level and total score and the achievements you unlock to Google Play Games. Your gamer name, avatar and scores appear on the public leaderboards, visible to other players. This data is stored and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and your Play Games privacy settings; we do not copy it to any server of ours.

4. Deleting your data

  • On this device: Settings → Reset progress clears all game data stored on the phone; uninstalling the Game also deletes it.
  • Leaderboards & achievements: open Google Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, choose Spark Chain and delete; you can also hide your profile there (Settings → Profile and privacy).
  • Or email us at pitchcraftlabs@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and we will help within 30 days.

5. Your choices

  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional — you never have to watch one to keep playing.

6. Device features

The announcer voice uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The Game may use vibration. No audio is recorded and nothing is sent to us.

7. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention

Progress stays on your device until you use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the Game. Leaderboard and achievement data is kept by Google Play Games until you delete it (section 4). Data held by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

9. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by Android’s app sandbox. Ad traffic is encrypted in transit by the ads SDK.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published in the Game and on our store listing with a new effective date.
